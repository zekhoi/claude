# Provenance

All content in this registry is original to this repository. No third-party skills, rules, agents, or guidelines are bundled.

Content reused from elsewhere must declare `provenance` in its `registry/index.json` entry: the source repository URL, an exact commit or tag (never a branch), the upstream path, the license identifier, the registry-relative file holding any required notices, and a summary of local modifications. Do not bundle material whose redistribution rights are unclear; link to it instead.
